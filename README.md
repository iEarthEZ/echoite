# Echoite

Echoite is a minecraft java edition mod that introduces a brand-new ore along with unique items, powerful equipment and special abilities to make surviving and beating the game more exciting.

You can watch the showcase at https://youtu.be/xW1VAL9w2SY **OR** read below for indepth details.

## Features

**// Base**

- Echoite. The base material that has influence around all of the mod.
- Raw Echoite. The raw version that drops from mining the "Echoite Ore".
- Echoite Ore. That generates throughout the world. (Can be mined with iron pickaxe and above)
- Echoite Block. Made with 9 "Echoite" and is used for decoration purposes or as you may like.

**// Armor**

- Echoite Helmet (crafted with 5 echoite)
- Echoite Chestplate (crafted with 8 echoite)
- Echoite Leggings (crafted with 7 echoite)
- Echoite Boots (crafted with 4 echoite)

Requiring 24 Echoite for full set of armor.

Upon obtaining the full set the player gets special abilities. That includes basic vanilla effects too. The player gets strength 2 and regeneration 2 after wearing the full armor set and the boot gives the player the ability to hold space to fly up to 3 blocks. This can be handy and i think is more unique than double jump.

- Echoite Horse Armor (armor for horse)

**// Tools/Weapons**

- Echoite Sword. (deals 11 damage, short cooldown, strikes down enemies and nearby upmost 4 enemies max. # The lightning does NOT damage the player whatsoever).
- Echoite Pickaxe. (a unique pickaxe that breaks blocks in a 3x3 or 9 block radius. mining the block will mine the surrounding 8 blocks too. thus it can be also avoided by shifting(crouching) and then mining the block).
- Echoite Axe. (a standard axe i guess)
- Echoite Shovel (shovel)
- Echoite Hoe (hoe)

- Echoite Bow. (this is where stuff gets juicy. a bow that does NOT require any type or kind of projectiles and shoots ghast fireballs and creating a small explosion on impact. it also gives the player recoil. with enough safety the bow can be used to climb certain heights by shooting the fireball downwards giving the player a boost also exploiting the recoil feature)

**// Food and Fuels**

- Echoite Carrots. (to survive all the chaos and grind lovers. craftable with 8 echoite blocks and 1 carrot. it gives regen and max saturation)
- Echoi Berry. (berries that spawn in the world randomly like sweet berries but more common and bit more powerful)

- Ecoalhite. (alternative to coal, just mine some coal and mix it with echoite in crafting grid to get ecoalhite. also it is 2x better than charcoal and more efficient).

## Installation

1. Install 1.21 of Minecraft Java Edition.
2. Install Fabric Loader for your Minecraft version.
3. Download and install Fabric API.
4. Download the Echoite mod's `.jar` file.
5. Place the `.jar` file in your Minecraft `mods` folder.
6. Launch Minecraft using the Fabric profile.
