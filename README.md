# Echoite

INTRODUCING A BRAND NEW ORE TO MINECRAFT! The ECHOITE (pronounced AE-KO-ITE) is a twist to add to your minecraft world. It has its ups and downs! It is similar to the iron but more powerful in its abilities. The durability is as similar to the existing iron, BUT the overpowerness reaches up to netherite! It also gives you strength 2 and regeneration 3 effects as long as you wear the full set of armor! The tools on the other hand has less cooldown and more damage than regular tools!

# Features
- A new material called the echoite!
- echoite ore can generate throughout the world like copper, iron and other ores
- the raw echoite drops from mining echoite ore with atleast an iron pickaxe
- the raw echoite can be smelt in furnace or blast furnace to get "echoite"
- the echoite can be used to make block of echoite, echoite helmet, echoite chestplate, echoite leggings, echoite boots, echoite sword, echoite axe, echoite pickaxe, echoite shovel, echoite hoe.
- wearing a full set of echoite armor gives strength 2 and regeneration 3!
- all echoite tools are revamped to be more overpowered and useful than regular tools.
